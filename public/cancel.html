Cancellation and refunds

Cancel any time. No runaround.

Effective October 15, 2026 · The Lazy Creator Company, Guelph, ON, Canada

Monthly plans

Solo, Agency and extra-business plans can be cancelled at any time, in a couple of taps:

  1. Go to your dashboard and choose Manage billing.
  2. Choose Cancel plan.

Your plan keeps working until the end of the month you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again. We don't give partial refunds for the rest of a month that's already started.

Lifetime (pay once) plans

You can ask for a refund within 7 days of buying a lifetime plan. We refund 70% of the price you paid; The Lazy Creator Company keeps 30% to cover payment fees and setup. After 7 days, lifetime plans can't be refunded.

Solo lifetime ($299 CAD): refund of $209.30 CAD

Agency lifetime ($999 CAD): refund of $699.30 CAD

To ask for a refund, email thelazycreatorco@gmail.com from your account's email address with "Refund request" in the subject. The refund goes back to the card you paid with (your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it), and the plan ends when it's refunded.

New website deposits

Deposits and final payments for new websites follow your written quote. Questions about a payment? Email thelazycreatorco@gmail.com.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account from your dashboard cancels any monthly plan straight away and deletes your information. If you've bought a lifetime plan and want a refund, ask within the 7 days before you delete.

Emails and unsubscribing

HindSited never sends newsletters or marketing. It only emails you about your account (confirming your email, password resets, receipts and your plan) and the alerts you set up, like new quote requests or private feedback from your review page.

  • To stop alerts for a review page or calculator, change or remove the notification email in that tool, or delete the tool.
  • To stop everything, delete your account from your dashboard.

Questions

Email thelazycreatorco@gmail.com. You'll hear back from a real person.
